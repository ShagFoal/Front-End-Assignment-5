Key Landmarks

Baiterek

A good starting point for a walk through central Astana. Look up at its golden sphere and explore the boulevard.

  • Central location
  • Panoramic observation floor
  • Memorable photo stop

Khan Shatyr

This tent-shaped building brings shopping, food and entertainment together into one massive climate-controlled venue.

  • Shopping and dining gallery
  • Indoor beach resort
  • Unique architectural feat

National Museum

Dedicated to preserving and displaying Kazakhstan's rich cultural, historical, and ethnographical legacy.

  • Hall of Ancient and Medieval History
  • Hall of Gold artefacts
  • Modern Kazakhstan exhibition
